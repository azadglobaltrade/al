The procurement problem

What an unreliable bag supplier really costs you

Most sourcing risk isn't the bag itself — it's what happens between approving a sample and receiving the container. Here's how each failure mode is closed on our orders.

The bulk run doesn't match the approved sample

GSM drifts, stitching shortcuts appear, print colours shift. On our orders the approved sample and a written specification are pinned to the proforma invoice, and production is checked against that spec batch by batch.

The container misses your retail window

A late delivery wipes out the season you ordered for. You receive an indicative schedule of 20–35 days confirmed in writing before any deposit, so you can plan inward logistics against real dates rather than hopeful ones.

Paperwork surprises at customs

A missing certificate delays discharge and racks up demurrage. Every consignment ships with its complete documentation set — invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and phytosanitary certificates where applicable — with drafts shared for your approval first.

Green claims you can't defend

Vague “eco” marketing is now a regulatory liability in the UK and EU. We describe jute factually — a annually renewable, plant-fibre packaging material — and supply batch test reports on request, so the claims you make downstream stay defensible.

Every mechanism above can be written into your order confirmation — ask us and we'll put it in writing.

Wholesale FAQ

The questions procurement teams actually ask

Straight answers on minimums, samples, lead times and payment. Anything not covered belongs in your quote enquiry — it gets a written reply.

What is your minimum order quantity for jute bags?

Trial orders may be available below standard MOQs so you can prove demand before committing to container volumes. Exact minimums depend on bag size, fabric GSM and printing — send your specification for a same-day answer.

How quickly will I receive a quotation?

Our target is a full quotation within 12 minutes. Including quantity, approximate size, destination port and any artwork in your first message makes this faster.

Can I see physical samples before committing?

Yes. Samples are typically dispatched within 3–7 business days. Courier charges depend on destination and are often adjusted against your first production order.

Can you produce bags in our own branded design?

Yes — private-label production with 1–4 colour screen printing, woven labels and swing tags. Share artwork and we confirm feasibility and pricing within 12 minutes.

Which sizes and fabric weights can you manufacture?

Construction is fully custom. Typical jute fabric weights run 250–350 GSM, laminated or unlaminated, with rope, cane, padded cotton or flat-tape handles.

What are your production lead times?

Indicative lead time is 20–35 days from proforma confirmation, depending on quantity and print complexity. An exact schedule is confirmed in writing before production starts.

Which shipping terms do you offer?

FOB Kolkata, CIF, DDP and Ex-Works are available. Frequently served European ports include Felixstowe, Rotterdam and Hamburg. Every consignment ships with its complete documentation set.

How do payments work for first-time buyers?

Typically 30% advance with the balance against scanned shipping documents; letters of credit at sight are available for established buyers. Final terms are agreed per proforma invoice.

Are your jute bags suitable for import into the UK?

Yes. Our jute bags are exported regularly to the United Kingdom and comply with UK import requirements. We provide a valid commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin (UK form A available for GSP where applicable) and any phytosanitary or customs documentation needed. Bags are shipped to Felixstowe, Southampton and other major UK ports on request.

Do you supply jute bags directly to German wholesalers and importers?

Yes — we work directly with German importers, wholesalers and retail chains from our West Bengal base. Shipments go to Hamburg, Bremerhaven, Rotterdam and other North Sea / Baltic ports with full EU customs documentation (EUR.1 certificate of origin, commercial invoice, packing list). German-speaking sales contact available on request.

What are the import requirements for jute packaging in the Netherlands?

Jute bags for the Dutch market require a commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin. As India is outside the EU, we issue an EUR.1 or equivalent to qualify for preferential tariff treatment under the EU-India trade arrangement. Phytosanitary certificates are provided for food-contact or agricultural applications. We ship to Rotterdam, Amsterdam and other NL ports regularly.

Are your jute bags biodegradable and eco-friendly for European buyers?

Yes — natural jute fibre is 100 % biodegradable and compostable in home or industrial composting environments. It has a significantly lower carbon footprint than plastic packaging and meets the environmental claims expected by UK, German and Dutch consumers. We can supply test reports confirming biodegradability and fibre content on request for your sustainability reporting.

How are jute bag prices structured for UK and EU B2B buyers?

Pricing is quoted EXW, FOB Kolkata, CIF to your destination port or DDP to your warehouse. All prices are in USD unless agreed otherwise and include the written specification sheet. For UK and EU buyers we include freight, insurance and import duties in CIF/DDP quotes so there are no hidden costs at customs. Volume discounts apply — request an exact quote with your quantity band and destination.

Can you help with customs clearance and duty for first-time EU importers?

We provide a complete shipping document set (commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, bill of lading / airway bill) and can advise on HS code 6302 (jute bags) duty rates for the UK, Germany and the Netherlands. For first-time importers we recommend our DDP term which delivers to your door with all duties and taxes pre-paid, removing customs surprises entirely.
