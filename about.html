Our Story

Trade, the way it should be done.

Azad Global Trade started with a simple frustration: too much of India's export trade runs through layers of intermediaries who add cost without adding value, and too many buyers overseas learn about missing paperwork only after their container is stuck at customs.

We built the opposite — a lean export house that sources directly from farms and processing units across West Bengal, tests and certifies every batch before it's packed, and treats documentation as part of the product rather than an afterthought.

Today that approach ships spices, grains, pulses, oilseeds, handicrafts, and eco-friendly goods to buyers in 3 countries — each consignment backed by the same licenses, lab reports, and certificates our compliance desk is happy to show you before you order.

FAQ

Common questions about us

Where is Azad Global Trade based?
We're based in Debkunda, West Bengal, India — where our procurement, quality testing, and export documentation are all handled in-house, close to the farms and processing units we source from.
What products do you export?
Spices, grains and pulses, oilseeds, handicrafts, and eco-friendly products — each backed by the licenses, quality certificates, and shipping paperwork your customs broker will expect to see. Browse our full documentation library for details.
How long has Azad Global Trade been exporting?
We've grown steadily since registering as an export house — from our first shipments in the Middle East and Southeast Asia to buyers across 3 countries today, adding certifications and lab partnerships along the way.
Can I get references from your existing buyers?
Yes. Message our compliance desk on WhatsApp or email and we'll arrange a verification call or share references from buyers in a comparable market to yours.
How do I become a buyer?
Reach out via WhatsApp or email with the product and destination country you're interested in. We'll confirm what's in season, share relevant certificates, and quote a sample order.
