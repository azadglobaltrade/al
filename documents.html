FAQ

Common questions from buyers

How do I request a copy of a document?
Click any document card to open its details, then use the "Request Copy" button — it opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to our compliance team. We respond within 12 minutes with a PDF and verification link.
Are these documents valid in my country?
Yes. Our IEC, GST, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary, Fumigation, and Lab reports are issued by the relevant Government of India bodies (DGFT, Plant Quarantine, NABL labs) and are accepted across export markets including the EU, USA, Middle East, and Southeast Asia.
Do you provide documents before I place the order?
Our standing licenses (IEC, ISO, GST) are available immediately. Per-consignment documents (Phytosanitary, Lab Report, BOL) are issued once your order is confirmed and goods are packed — but you can request a sample certificate of the same type beforehand.
Can my buyer's broker verify these directly?
Absolutely. Each certificate carries the issuing authority's reference number and a QR/verification link where applicable. Your buyer's customs broker can independently confirm authenticity with the issuing body.
What if my destination country needs extra paperwork?
Tell us the destination port and product — we'll pre-check the import requirements of your specific country and flag any additional certificates (e.g. Health Certificate, Certificate of Free Sale, Radiation testing) we can arrange through partner labs.
