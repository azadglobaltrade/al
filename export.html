01

Compliance & Licensing

Azad Global Trade operates as a registered Indian exporter holding a valid Importer-Exporter Code (IEC) issued by DGFT. Every consignment is cleared through authorised customs channels in accordance with India’s Foreign Trade Policy, the Customs Act, and all applicable export control regulations.

02

Product Scope

We export spices, grains & pulses, oilseeds, handicrafts, and eco-friendly products sourced directly from growers and artisan clusters. Goods are accurately classified under the correct Harmonized System (HS) codes, and declarations reflect the true nature, quantity, and value of every shipment.

03

Quality Assurance

Every lot undergoes pre-shipment inspection and NABL-accredited laboratory testing for purity, moisture, and contaminant levels. Agricultural consignments are phytosanitary-cleared before dispatch. Batches that fail specification are rejected at source — never shipped to compensate for shortfalls.

04

Export Documentation

A complete document set accompanies every order: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, Lab Test Report, Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, and Insurance Certificate where applicable. Documents are couriered to the buyer or presented to the nominated bank as per the agreed payment term.

05

Payments & Banking

All export proceeds are routed through our Authorised Dealer bank in compliance with FEMA and RBI realisation timelines. We accept advance remittance, Letters of Credit, and documents-against-payment arrangements. Under-invoicing, over-invoicing, or third-party settlement requests are strictly declined.

06

Shipping & Logistics

Shipments move on FOB, CIF, or agreed Incoterms from Indian ports. Packing is ISPM-15 compliant with fumigated pallets, food-grade liners where required, and sealed containers documented in the shipping bill. Cargo insurance is arranged when the contract calls for it.

07

Restricted & Prohibited Goods

We do not export items on the DGFT negative list, protected flora and fauna, dual-use goods, hazardous chemicals, or any commodity requiring special authorisation we do not hold. We likewise decline shipments to destinations subject to UN or applicable government sanctions.

08

Ethical Sourcing

We buy directly from farmers, FPOs, and artisan families at fair prices — no exploitative middle layers. Our supply chain prohibits child and forced labour, and we prioritise jute, bamboo, and other biodegradable materials that keep consignments environmentally responsible from field to port.

09

Policy Updates

This policy is reviewed periodically and updated to reflect changes in law, licensing, or internal process. The version published here supersedes all earlier versions. Questions about this policy can be directed to azadglobaltrade@gmail.com.
